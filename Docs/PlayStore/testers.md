# Closed test: recruiting testers

Google requires a personal developer account to run a closed test with at least 12
testers opted in for 14 days in a row before it can apply for production. Aim for 20 or
more to cover dropouts.

## Setup

- Track: Play Console → JS8 Firefly → Test and release → Closed testing → "Closed testing - Alpha",
  release 2 (1.0), live since Nov 14, 2026.
- Testers come from the Google Group "JS8 Firefly Testers"
  (`js8firefly-testers@googlegroups.com`). Anyone can join without approval; only the
  owner can post or see the member list. This replaced the `testersJS8Firefly` email list
  on Nov 21, 2026; the four people on that list have to join the group to keep access.
- The track's "Feedback URL or email address" field is empty. Google asks about tester
  feedback on the production application, so fill it in.

## Links for testers

Both steps, in this order, with the same Google account:

1. Join the group: https://groups.google.com/g/js8firefly-testers/about
2. Opt in and install: https://play.google.com/apps/testing/com.js8firefly.app

## Applying for production

Once 12 testers have been opted in for 14 days, the Dashboard offers "Apply for
production". It asks how testers were recruited, what feedback they gave and what
changed because of it, so keep notes on reports as they come in.
